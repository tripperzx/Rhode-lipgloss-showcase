the formula

Glass, not glitter

Rhode Lip Gloss is built on a peptide lip treatment base — hydrating, non-sticky, and buildable from a sheer wash of color to a fuller pigmented finish. No feathering, no tackiness, just glass-like shine that holds up through a full day.

the applicator

Precision, not mess

A soft doe-foot applicator delivers an even, controlled layer every time — no overloading, no drag. Designed for quick reapplication on the go, straight from your bag.
